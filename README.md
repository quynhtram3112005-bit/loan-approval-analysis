## Business Questions
- Which factors have the greatest impact on loan approval?
- How does Debt-to-Income Ratio affect approval rates?
- Which customer segments have the highest approval potential?

## Key Findings
- Debt-to-Income Ratio is the strongest approval driver.
- Applicants earning above $70K have a 61.41% approval rate.
- Prime Borrowers achieve a 73.71% approval rate.
